What We Do
Full HVAC Services
Wallace Atwell and his team handle everything from routine tune-ups to full system installs. Click a category to see details.
Central AC Installation
Full central air conditioning system installs for new construction or replacements. Properly sized for your home's square footage.
Furnace & Heat Pump Installation
Gas furnace and electric heat pump installations with proper load calculations and ductwork connections.
Mini-Split Systems 🌭
Ductless mini-split installation for additions, garages, sunrooms, or any space without existing ductwork.
Thermostat Upgrades 🌡
Smart thermostat installation including Nest, Ecobee, and Honeywell — schedule your comfort and save on energy bills.
System Replacement
Old unit costing you money? We'll swap it for a high-efficiency model and haul away the old equipment.
Spring & Fall Tune-Ups
Seasonal checkups that clean coils, check refrigerant, test electrical components, and ensure peak efficiency before heavy use.
Filter Replacement & Coil Cleaning
Dirty filters and coils reduce efficiency by up to 40%. We clean and replace to keep your system running like new.
Refrigerant Level Check
Low refrigerant means higher bills and a struggling unit. We check levels and recharge as needed.
Ductwork Inspection
Leaky ducts can waste 20–30% of conditioned air. We inspect and seal to maximize the air you're paying for.
Maintenance Advice
Wallace takes the time to walk you through how to care for your system and avoid costly breakdowns — no pressure, just honest advice.
AC Not Cooling
Compressor issues, low refrigerant, frozen coils — we diagnose fast and get you cool same day when possible.
Heating System Failure
From cracked heat exchangers to faulty igniters, we repair gas furnaces and heat pumps quickly and correctly.
Electrical & Capacitor Repairs
Bad capacitors, contactors, and wiring issues are some of the most common HVAC failures — we fix them affordably.
Emergency Service Calls
System out in extreme heat or cold? We prioritize emergency calls — especially for families with infants or elderly residents.
Mini-Split Repairs
Ductless unit not cooling or heating a room? We service all major mini-split brands including Gree, Mitsubishi, and LG.
