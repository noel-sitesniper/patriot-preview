What We Do
Full HVAC Services
Wallace Atwell and his team handle everything from routine tune-ups to full system installs. Click a category to see details.
AC & Furnace Split System
Full split system installations combining an outdoor condenser with an indoor air handler or furnace. Properly sized and charged for maximum efficiency and long-term comfort in your home or building.
Gas Furnace
New gas furnace installations for reliable, cost-effective heating. We handle everything from load calculations to final testing so your home stays warm all winter long.
Air Ducts
Complete ductwork installation for new construction or system replacements. Properly designed duct systems ensure even airflow throughout your space and prevent energy waste.
Heater
Installation of electric and gas heaters for residential and light commercial applications. Whether it's a wall unit, baseboard, or central system, we get it done right.
Air Ducts
Routine duct inspections and cleaning to remove buildup, seal leaks, and ensure your system is delivering clean, conditioned air to every room efficiently. Dirty or leaky ducts can waste 20–30% of your heating and cooling energy.
AC & Furnace Split System
Diagnosing and repairing split system failures — from compressor and refrigerant issues to blower motor and control board problems. We get your system back up and running fast.
Electric Furnace
Repair of electric furnaces including heating element replacement, sequencer issues, thermostat faults, and blower problems. We restore consistent heat quickly and affordably.
Heater
All types of heater repairs — wall units, space heaters, and central systems. If it's not producing heat or cycling correctly, we'll find the problem and fix it right the first time.
Air Ducts
Repairing collapsed, disconnected, or leaking ductwork that's reducing airflow and driving up your energy bill. We locate the problem areas and seal or replace damaged sections.
Gas Furnace
Gas furnace repairs including igniter replacement, heat exchanger inspection, gas valve issues, and burner cleaning. We diagnose safely and repair to manufacturer standards.
